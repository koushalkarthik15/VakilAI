## 1. PREMISES
The Landlord hereby leases to the Tenant the residential property located at Flat 201, Green View Residency, Gachibowli, Hyderabad, Telangana 500032 (the "Demised Premises"), comprising 2 Bedrooms, 1 Hall, and 1 Kitchen.

## 2. TERM OF LEASE
The term of this lease shall be for a period of 11 (eleven) months commencing from the 1st day of October 2026 and expiring on the 31st day of August 2027. 

## 3. RENT AND DEPOSIT
**3.1 Monthly Rent:** The Tenant shall pay a monthly rent of INR 35,000 (Rupees Thirty-Five Thousand only). The rent must be paid on or before the 5th day of every calendar month.
**3.2 Late Fee:** In the event the rent is not paid by the 5th of the month, the Tenant agrees to pay a late penalty of INR 2,000 per day until the rent is paid in full.
**3.3 Security Deposit:** The Tenant has paid a security deposit of INR 2,10,000 (Rupees Two Lakh Ten Thousand only), equivalent to 6 months' rent, at the time of signing this Agreement. This deposit shall be refunded at the end of the lease term without interest, subject to deductions for any damages to the property.

## 4. MAINTENANCE AND UTILITIES
The Tenant shall be responsible for paying all electricity, water, and internet bills directly to the respective authorities. The monthly maintenance charge of INR 3,000 to the housing society shall also be borne by the Tenant.

## 5. REPAIRS AND DAMAGES
The Tenant agrees to keep the premises in good and habitable condition. Any major structural repairs shall be the responsibility of the Landlord. However, any minor repairs (under INR 5,000) shall be borne by the Tenant.

## 6. TERMINATION
**6.1 Lock-in Period:** There is a strict lock-in period of 6 months. If the Tenant vacates the premises before the lock-in period expires, the entire security deposit will be forfeited.
**6.2 Notice Period:** After the lock-in period, either party may terminate this Agreement by giving a 1 (one) month written notice.

## 7. USE OF PREMISES
The Demised Premises shall be used strictly for residential purposes only. The Tenant shall not use the property for any commercial, illegal, or anti-social activities. Subletting is strictly prohibited.

## 8. JURISDICTION
Any dispute arising out of this Agreement shall be subject to the exclusive jurisdiction of the courts in Hyderabad, Telangana.
